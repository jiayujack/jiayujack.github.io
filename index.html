Ph.D. student · Computer Science & Engineering

Currently a Research Intern at Unity Game AI, evaluating multimodal LLMs for gameplay bug detection. Previously an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon.

Research

Current projects

Three directions connecting inference efficiency, learning under uncertainty, and interpretable financial signals.

LLM servingUnder review

RebaseKV

Hiding prefill latency in multi-agent LLMs

Multi-agent workflows repeatedly spend time preparing related prompts.

Approach. Begin optimistic decoding with inherited KV states while exact prompt prefill runs asynchronously, then align the cache.

KV-cache reuseMulti-agent inference

Post-training & RLUnder review

Beta-RL

Learning how to combine multiple rewards

Reward signals differ in reliability, making fixed aggregation weights difficult to choose.

Approach. Model reward uncertainty and learn Bayesian weights for multi-reward reinforcement learning of LLMs.

Bayesian learningMulti-reward RL
Manuscript details

Business news as economic signals

Large news archives contain changing narratives that are difficult to measure consistently.

Approach. Use LLM-refined topic clustering to turn business news into interpretable signals for economic and financial forecasting.
